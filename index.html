Getting Here

Welcome to Monkey River

Monkey River Village sits at the mouth of the river in the Toledo District, reached by road and boat from Placencia and Independence. Meet your captain at the dock and we handle the rest, from life jackets to the cooler on board.

Getting Around

Getting around Belize by water

Arriving is only the beginning. Our covered, open-air boats glide through mangrove-lined rivers, across shallow flats and out to the cayes, with local captains who know every channel and every troop of howlers.

Protected Waters

World Heritage waters of Belize

The Belize Barrier Reef sits just miles off the coast and is part of the larger Mesoamerican Barrier Reef. Home to hundreds of species of coral and fish, it is a hot spot for snorkelers, divers and biologists from all over the world. We follow strict leave-no-trace rules on every trip.

Our Story

Relax with us on the water

Relax Tours & Charters is a Belizean boat tour company serving Monkey River Village, Placencia and the cayes of southern Belize. Most of our guests first find us through friends and word of mouth, and many come back year after year.

Where to find us: we depart from Monkey River Village and the Placencia lagoon on Belize’s southern coast. We will send exact meeting directions with your booking confirmation.
